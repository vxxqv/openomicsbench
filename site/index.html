01 / What it does

A test collection with an audit trail.

OpenOmicsBench lets you validate bundled data, inspect its source and transformations, score your own outputs, and compare methods across runs. Result manifests lock input hashes; reports and evidence packages make a run easier to review or reproduce.

The 12 biological bulk RNA-seq objects come from seven Expression Atlas studies. The other fixtures are declared synthetic tests for sequences, variants, peptide identification, single-cell clustering and chromatin peaks. They are software test cases, not clinical reference materials.

03 / Get started

From install to report in two commands.

The demo scores bundled reference answers against their own truth sets. It shows how the evaluator works; it is not a measurement of your method. Replace the example result files with your own outputs when you are ready to test a pipeline.

Full usage guide ↗
