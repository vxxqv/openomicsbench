01 / Single-cell

Separate cluster structure from cell names.

Compare cell clusters with a declared reference, then inspect exact annotation labels and embedding neighborhoods. The report shows biological-state preservation and batch mixing side by side.

omicsbench cell assess singlecell-002 clusters.tsv --annotation labels.tsv --embedding coordinates.tsv

Need H5AD input? cell export-h5ad can export chosen AnnData fields with an optional dependency. Six sourced CellBench cases cover three platforms and three technical plates from one study. The separate singlecell-001 case is synthetic. These labels support comparisons within the declared cases; they do not establish general cell-type accuracy.

02 / Small variants

Count only calls inside evaluable regions.

omicsbench variant benchmark truth.vcf.gz calls.vcf.gz regions.bed.gz --assembly GRCh38 --truth-assembly GRCh38

The report separates SNVs, indels, and excluded calls. It uses exact allele representations. For a germline performance claim, compare the matching GIAB truth VCF, BED and reference with a representation-aware tool such as hap.py.

03 / Chromatin

Inspect peak sets and replicate agreement.

omicsbench interval assess peaks.narrowPeak --replicate second.narrowPeak --reference reference.narrowPeak

Peak widths, reciprocal overlap and summit distance are reported separately. FRiP, TSS enrichment and IDR summaries can be imported from existing analyses; this tool does not calculate them from reads or replace an ENCODE pipeline.

04 / Proteomics

Keep identification and quantity distinct.

omicsbench peptide assess reference.tsv identifications.tsv --q 0.001 --q 0.01 --q 0.05

The report compares protein-peptide pairs across q-value thresholds and reports quantitative error only when both tables provide positive quantities. It does not reprocess spectra or resolve protein inference.
